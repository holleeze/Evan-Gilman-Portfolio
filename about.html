Hey, I'm Evan.

I'm a video producer and content creator with a passion for gaming, esports, and making people smile.

Currently at Team Liquid, I produce social content, talent interviews, brand campaigns, and documentary-style series. I've worked on everything from viral TikToks to the SQUAD docuseries presented by Alienware.

What drives me is the challenge of capturing authentic moments and turning them into content that resonates. Whether it's a street interview that hits 2 million views or an intimate behind-the-scenes look at pro players, I'm always chasing that next idea that'll grab people's attention.

When I'm not behind a camera, I'm probably gaming, watching esports, or thinking about the next shoot.
